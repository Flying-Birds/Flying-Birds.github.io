As the old year gave way to the new and winter softened towards spring, our eighth event took a different form from our usual in-person support gatherings: a special reunion before Chinese New Year.

Looking back over the previous six months, we had grown from a scattered handful of people into a community taking shape; from the easy conversation of informal chats to our first structured peer support circle. The journey had brought uncertainty and experimentation, but also countless moments of warmth and healing. None of this steady progress was ever the result of my persistence alone.

A group of gentle people had quietly accompanied, accepted and supported me, helping this small community grow. Our Chinese New Year meal therefore had no schedule, no discussion topic and no task to complete. There was only good food, lively conversation and the warmth of gathering together—a sincere expression of gratitude.

The path of organising charitable events is not an easy one. At many points I became trapped in self-doubt, exhaustion and uncertainty. Yet these friends continued to make room for my emotions, sustain my original purpose and help the group move steadily forward. They accepted my imperfections and believed in the reason I had begun. When my strength failed, they quietly held everything up. At every gathering, they offered their help and protected this warm little home for the spirit.

The finest relationships are those in which our values align and our hearts move to the same rhythm. The rarest support is companionship offered without expectation of return. We met through a desire to heal and came to know one another through gentleness. There was no elaborate social ceremony and no hierarchy of status—only mutual understanding and a willingness to catch one another when we fell, preserving kindness and companionship within our small circle.

How fortunate we are to share the passing years. I would like to offer my heartfelt thanks to the friends who have remained beside me throughout this journey, listed in the order in which they joined the group: Cheese, Ninety, Long Shaonian, Lin, SpongeBob, Xiaolu, Chairman and Little Sister.

Thank you for six months of acceptance, affection and steadfast support. Thank you for choosing to stay, for trusting, accompanying and helping me, and for sustaining this gentle corner of the spirit. Thank you for embracing every imperfection along the way and for helping me safeguard a place where vulnerability is permitted and every emotion may be received.

Gathered around a table filled with warmth and flavour, we welcomed another peaceful year. This simple meal before the festival was both reunion and requital, conversation and gratitude flowing in both directions.

In the year ahead, may we remain true to our purpose and continue gently side by side.
